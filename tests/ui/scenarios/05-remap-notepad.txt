This binary is a test build: started with --accept-injected it also remaps input injected by other software, which is what lets your keystrokes reach its remapping. Without that flag the test cannot work, so pass it exactly as written.

1. Launch C:\Test\winremap.exe with the arguments: --config C:\Test\uitest.toml --lang en --accept-injected
2. Wait 5 seconds. Do not look for its window: it has none, it only sits in the notification area.

6. Press Ctrl+H once, as a real key chord sent to Notepad. Wait 2 seconds.
7. Read Notepad's text again and report it.
